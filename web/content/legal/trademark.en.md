The Insane OS code is free software under the MIT license, and you can do almost anything with it. The **Insane OS name**, the **apple logo** and **Lumo**, the mascot, are different: they tell people that something comes from the Insane OS project. This policy explains how you can use them so that nobody is misled.

## What the brand includes

- the name **Insane OS** and the wordmark (**Insane**OS, set in Plus Jakarta Sans);
- the apple logo, the app icon, the apple symbol and their variants;
- **Lumo**, the guardian of Insane OS, in every pose and drawing.

The official files and the rules for using them (clear space, colours, minimum sizes) are in the [brand kit](https://github.com/insaneos/Insane OS/blob/main/docs/INSANEOS-BRAND.md).

## What you can do without asking

- **Refer to the project** by name, for example "runs on Insane OS", "a theme for Insane OS" or "compatible with Insane OS".
- **Link** to insaneos.org or the repository, with the name or the unmodified logo.
- **Write about Insane OS**: articles, reviews, videos, tutorials, books, talks and classes, using screenshots, the name and the logo.
- **Use the logos and Lumo from the brand kit, unmodified**, following its rules: no stretching, recolouring, other fonts, effects or redrawing.
- **Share unmodified Insane OS images** (the ISO, the Raspberry Pi image or the `.deb`) as we publish them, for example on a mirror or a USB stick for a friend.
- **Make community material**, such as stickers or wallpapers for yourself or a user group, as long as you don't sell it.

## What needs our permission

- **Implying endorsement:** don't suggest that the Insane OS project or iPERFEX makes, sponsors, certifies or supports your product, service, company or event.
- **Modified distributions:** if you change Insane OS (other packages, code, settings or branding) and distribute the result, you can't call it Insane OS or use the apple logo or Lumo without written permission.
- **Commercial use of the brand:** selling merchandise with the logo or Lumo, or using them in advertising for a product.
- **Names and domains:** using "Insane OS" or something confusingly similar in the name of a product, company, app, distribution or domain name.
- **Modified logos or a modified Lumo**, or new logos that imitate them.

## Forks

Forks are welcome; that's what free software is for. A fork must have **its own name and its own logo**, different from Insane OS's and not confusingly similar, and must remove the apple logo, Lumo and the Insane OS wordmark from the system and its website. You may say, truthfully, that it is "based on Insane OS".

## Asking for permission

Write to us through [our contact form](https://www.iperfex.com/contacto/?lang=en) with what you want to do. We are usually glad to say yes to user groups, events and projects that help people use Insane OS.
